Denuvo's "Current" Version Hacked. Hacker Boasted About Their First Victory Over DRM Protection

NewsSource: Сообщество Total War Three Kingdom в Steam
15 October 2025, 05:35Boris Piletskiy

Translated with AI from the Russian original.

While Empress is in retreat, other tech enthusiasts are working. The protection of Total War Three Kingdoms has finally fallen.

Recently, players once again remembered the global strategy Total War Three Kingdoms. The reason was the hacker voices38, who announced the hacking of Denuvo. He clarified that this is the current version 1.7.1 and all DLCs are available.

The game itself is not very recent (the original version was released back in 2019), in this case the very fact of hacking Denuvo is important - the hacker said that he was researching the solutions of colleagues:

"I spent a lot of time reverse engineering Denuvo and studying old \"cracks\" to see how others were cracking DRM, since no one else seems to care about it anymore. Here's my first attempt".
