 4 I.       EACH REQUEST IS TARGETED TO IDENTIFYING AND SERVING

 5          DEFENDANT

 6          Courts permit limited early discovery “to permit the plaintiff to learn the identifying facts

 7 necessary to permit service on the defendant.” Columbia Ins. Co. v. seescandy.com, 185 F.R.D. 573,

 8 577 (N.D. Cal. 1999). The plaintiff must provide “a statement of reasons justifying the specific

 9 discovery requested.” Id. at 580.

10          A.     The Revised Requests Follow the Alo Subpoena.

11          The Order identifies the subpoena in Alo as the model for Denuvo’s requests. Dkt. No. 24 at
12 2. In Alo, Judge Kang granted leave to serve that subpoena, which sought “[a]ll DOCUMENTS

13 RELATING TO the contact information for the ebay seller kyy_40,” including “any identifying

14 information such as . . . names, identification documents, email addresses, business or residential

15 addresses.” Alo, 2026 U.S. Dist. LEXIS 133181, at *17; Alo, ECF No. 16-2 at 16.

16          Denuvo’s revised requests follow the same form. Each seeks all documents relating to the
17 identity or contact information of one account that the Complaint attributes to Defendant, including

18 names, identification documents, email addresses, and business or residential addresses. Exs. A-C.

19 Exhibit A addresses the Discord user “voices38,” User ID 1427416652684984397. Compl. ¶ 4.

20 Exhibit B addresses the Reddit user “voices38.” Id. ¶¶ 3, 64. Exhibit C contains a separate request

21 for each of the seven Steam profiles listed in the Complaint. Id. ¶ 5.

22          The requests do not seek the contents of any message, post, or file, which federal law

23 generally bars these providers from disclosing. 18 U.S.C. § 2702(a)(1). The requests seek records

24 “pertaining to a subscriber,” which a provider may disclose “to any person other than a

25 governmental entity.” Id. § 2702(c)(6). Measured against the Alo subpoena, Denuvo’s requests

26 differ in only three respects. They reach identity as well as contact information, and they add

27 telephone numbers and Internet Protocol (“IP”) addresses. Denuvo respectfully submits that these

28 differences are permissible in light of the Order and Alo.

 1             B.     Each Difference Is Needed to Identify Defendant and Aligns with Alo.

 2                    1.      Identity Information

 3             In Alo, the plaintiff already had a name and a street address from the return label on the

 4 packages it bought. Alo, 2026 U.S. Dist. LEXIS 133181, at *2-3. In granting leave, Judge Kang

 5 described the information sought as an email address and a residential or business address. Id. at

 6 *15. Denuvo has only a pseudonym. Compl. ¶ 21. Denuvo therefore needs records showing who

 7 holds each account, including any name or identification document on file, to name Defendant and

 8 effect service. See Fed. R. Civ. P. 4(e), (f).

 9                    2.      Telephone Numbers

10             Ebates struck telephone numbers because the plaintiff did not explain why it needed them.

11 Ebates, Inc. v. Does, No. 16-cv-01925-JST, 2016 U.S. Dist. LEXIS 59480, at *9 (N.D. Cal. May 3,

12 2016). Denuvo’s reason is specific to Discord, which asks each new user to “provide a way of

13 contacting you (such as an email address and/or phone number).” Discord Privacy Policy (eff. Sept.

14 29, 2025).1 Discord may also require “a verified phone number.” Id. A Discord account may

15 therefore carry a telephone number and no email address. That number identifies a carrier and a

16 subscriber of record, which may be the only route to a name and address.

17                    3.      IP Addresses
18             eBay requires every individual seller to provide a full name, an address, a date of birth, and
19 a Social Security number. Alo, ECF No. 16-2 at 7. The platforms here require far less, and what they

20 require is self-reported. Valve collects an “email address and country of residence” at registration.

21 Steam Privacy Policy (rev. Feb. 14, 2025).2 Discord requires a username, a password, a birthday,

22 and a means of contact. Discord Privacy Policy, supra. Both platforms log IP addresses. Steam

23 Privacy Policy, supra (stating that “our servers log your IP address”); Discord Privacy Policy, supra

24 (collecting “information like your IP address”). Those addresses may be the only reliable path to

25 Defendant.
