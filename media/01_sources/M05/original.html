Version 1.18
------------
 - Persian SOP increases spy action success chance (bribe, ssassination,
   steal technology and create incident)
 - When Persian GB comes close to an enemy building, he can see the 
   information inside
 - Chinese SOP: increase in sales by 30%
 - Celtic SOP: train celtic unit 50% faster, and 20% faster on other race
 - Roman SOP: train 10 combat level more initially
 - Roman GB magic now affect all human races
 - Norman SOP: town of all races decrease 7 loyalty when collecting tax
 - Indian SOP: build war machine 30% faster, in addition to faster 
   researching
 - Indian GB: increase damage done by tornado
 - Viking SOP: +15 reputation per year
 - Viking SOP: all units get berserker attack 25 combat level earlier
 - Carth SOP: increase food production by 100%
 - Japanese GB: decrease cost of hiring inn unit by 30%
 - Japanese SPU: triple shuriken, if Japanese Ninja Berserker is
    researched
 - Japanese SPU have a 30% chance evading a range attack
 - All GB decreases HP slower
 - for 3x3 range attack or larger, only center of the arrow can attack 
   town or building
 - Each kingdom/kwyzan can hire at least one caravan from market
 - Fryhtan Lair randomly breeds a fryhtan free of charge, chance depends
   on number of fryhtans of that species.
