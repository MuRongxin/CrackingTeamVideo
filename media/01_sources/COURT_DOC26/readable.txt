                                  13          Before the Court is Plaintiff’s Ex Parte Motion for Expedited Discovery to serve third-

                                  14   party subpoenas prior to the Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 26(f) conference. Dkt. No. 15. The

                                  15   Court GRANTS the ex parte motion for the reasons below.



Northern District of California
                                  16          Plaintiff does not know the identity of or have contact information for the Defendant and is

                                  17   therefore unable to properly effect service. Dkt. No. 15-1. In these circumstances, courts have

                                  18   granted plaintiffs the opportunity to conduct early discovery for the narrowly tailored purpose of

                                  19   identifying the defendant and determining where they may be served. See e.g., Gillespie v.

                                  20   Civiletti, 629 F.2d 637, 642 (9th Cir. 1980). Courts generally require the moving party to: “(1)

                                  21   identify the defendant with enough specificity to allow the Court to determine whether the

                                  22   defendant is a real person or entity who could be sued in federal court; (2) recount the steps taken

                                  23   to locate the defendant; (3) show that its action could survive a motion to dismiss; and (4) file a

                                  24   request for discovery with the Court identifying the persons or entities on whom discovery process

                                  25   might be served and for which there is a reasonable likelihood that the discovery process will lead

                                  26   to identifying information.” See, e.g., Columbia Ins. Co. v. seescandy.com, 185 F.R.D. 573, 578-

                                  27   580 (N.D. Cal. 1999); Alo, LLC v. Fang, No. 25-CV-11126-PHK, 2026 WL 1725893 * 4 (N.D.

                                  28   Cal. June 15, 2026); Ebates, Inc. v. Does, No. 16-CV-01925-JST, 2016 WL 2344199 * 1 (N.D.

                                   6   identify the Defendant and effect proper service.1
