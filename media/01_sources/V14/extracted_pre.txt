Pragmata is a unique, sci-fi action-adventure game from Capcom.

Diana, a young android, as they navigate a lunar facility taken

properly refined, can be used to create almost anything using
3D printers. When the facility suddenly falls silent, Hugh and
his team are dispatched to investigate...but then disaster strikes.
Hugh is saved by Diana, a lone android wandering the complex.
The two of them must join forces to find a way off the moon while
working their way through an AI-controlled facility bent on
stopping them.
